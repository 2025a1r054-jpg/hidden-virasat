Archival Standards

Sources and Academic Verification

The narratives presented in Hidden Virasat synthesize peer-reviewed regional historiographies, royal court records (such as the Gulabnama written by Diwan Kripa Ram in 1876), gazetteers published by the Dogra regime and British surveys, and verified field documentation by the Archaeological Survey of India (ASI) and Mubarak Mandi Heritage Society.

We strictly delineate between verified epigraphic facts (such as the 1822 Raj Tilak at Jia Pota, Akhnoor) and foundational folklore (such as the 9th-century peace legend of Raja Jamboo Lochan), celebrating intangible cultural heritage while preserving historical rigor.
