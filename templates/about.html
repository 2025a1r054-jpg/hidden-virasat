About Hidden Virasat

"Beyond the Famous. Into the Forgotten." — Reviving the historical consciousness and cultural pride of Jammu (Duggar Pradesh).

Academic Project Disclosure

Hidden Virasat is built as a college artificial intelligence and full-stack web systems capstone project. It is an independent educational platform designed to demonstrate intelligent travel recommendation algorithms, interactive GIS cartography, and cultural archival design. It is not an official government tourism portal or commercial booking agency.

Our Mission

Why Jammu Heritage Matters

For decades, millions of travelers have passed through Jammu merely as a transit railway station or airport on their way to Kashmir or the Vaishno Devi pilgrimage. In doing so, travelers frequently bypass one of India's richest yet least understood hill civilizations: Duggar Pradesh.

Jammu was the cradle of the Dogra dynasty, which unified the trans-Himalayan territories from the plains of Punjab to the high peaks of Ladakh and Gilgit. It gave birth to the daring, emotionally charged Basohli miniature paintings, the sacred Shaivite sanctuaries along the subterranean Devika River, and the unique pastoral delicacy of Kalari cheese.

Hidden Virasat exists to illuminate these lesser-known treasures, encouraging responsible, culturally conscious travel that directly benefits local artisans, family-run food makers, and regional heritage preservation.

Algorithm Transparency

How Trip Planning & Recommendations Work

In alignment with ethical AI engineering principles, our recommendation system avoids opaque black-box operations and instead implements a transparent, multi-factor heuristic recommendation engine:

1

Interest & Category Weighting

Places in SQLite are scored based on semantic tags and category alignments matching the user's selected interests (Heritage, Spirituality, Food, Shopping, Crafts, or Nature).

2

Haversine Geographic Clustering

Using exact latitude and longitude coordinates, the algorithm calculates great-circle distances between stops to group proximate landmarks into the same day, preventing crisscrossing travel.

3

Pacing & Time Slot Allocation

Stops are assigned to realistic morning, midday, afternoon, and evening slots based on typical visit durations (e.g. 2.5 hours for Mubarak Mandi vs. 1 hour for Ranbireshwar Temple).

Technical Scope

Current Limitations & Future Roadmap

Current MVP Scope

  • Curated core of 15 verified landmarks and experiences across Jammu province.
  • Direct Open-Meteo satellite weather integration with in-memory caching.
  • Persistent local SQLite trip storage without requiring external user logins.

Future Horizons

  • Audio storytelling in Dogri, Hindi, and English recorded by local elders.
  • Integration of live Jammu Ropeway and local e-bus transit schedules.
  • Community artisan marketplace enabling direct booking of block printing ateliers.
